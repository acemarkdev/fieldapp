FitAWindow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

FitAWindow (the “app”) is the field and production app of ACE Group and Acemark Sp. z o.o. (“we”, “us”). It is used by our employees and contractors to survey, install and produce windows and doors, together with our office system at office.acemark.com.pl. This policy explains what personal data the app handles and why.

Who can use the app

The app is a business tool. Accounts are created by our administrators for our staff and contractors; you cannot sign up in the app. Anyone can open the built-in demo, which uses sample data only.

What we collect

  • Account data — your name, work email address and role, and a sign-in record. You sign in with a password set by us or with your Microsoft work account.
  • Work data you enter — jobs, windows and doors, measurements, notes, installation status and snags, floor plans and the positions of items on them.
  • Photos — photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library to document a survey or an installation. The app only uses the camera and photo library when you choose to add a photo.
  • Production time — for production staff, the tasks you start and stop, their start and end times, and the comments you add.
  • Demo and quote requests — if you open the demo or ask for a quote, the email address you enter and, if you give them, your name, company, phone number and message, so our sales team can contact you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data or browsing history. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we do not sell personal data.

Why we use it

To run our survey, installation and production work: to plan and record jobs, to document quality, to calculate job costs and pay, and to manage who may see and change what. Demo and quote details are used only to answer your request. The legal basis is our contract with you or our legitimate interest in running our business; for demo requests, your request.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored by our service providers acting on our instructions: Supabase (database and file storage), Render (our office server), Microsoft (sign-in with a work account) and monday.com (our job boards). Access inside the company is limited by role.

How long we keep it

Work records and photos are kept for as long as we need them for the job, its warranty and our legal and accounting obligations. Accounts are deactivated when someone stops working with us. Demo and quote requests are deleted when no longer needed for the request.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to its use or ask for a copy, under the GDPR / UK GDPR. To have an account removed, ask your manager or write to us. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Poland the UODO, in the UK the ICO).
