FitAWindow — Support

FitAWindow is the field and production app of ACE Group and Acemark Sp. z o.o., for our surveyors, fitters and production staff.

Getting an account

Accounts are created by our office. If you work with us and need access, ask your manager. Sign in with the email and password you were given, or with your Microsoft work account.

Trying it out

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and open the demo to explore the app with sample data. Nothing you do in the demo is saved.
